Privacy policy

What happens to your data on sonsoh.com, in plain language. The short version: we only collect what you type into our forms, we set no cookies and we never sell your data.

Last updated 24 November 2026

Who we are

sonsoh.com is the website of SONSOH, the maker of SONSOH NIGHT sleep earbuds. SONSOH is responsible for the personal data collected through this site. For any question or request about your data, use our contact form.

What we collect and why

Founders Batch reservations

When you reserve a Founders number we receive your name, your email address, the finish you chose and the page you sent the form from. We use this to hold your number and to email you about your reservation, your payment link and shipping. Reserving costs nothing: there are no payments on sonsoh.com itself.

The Nightlist

When you join the Nightlist we receive your email address and the page you signed up on. We use it to send you early access and launch news about SONSOH NIGHT. Ask us through the contact form and we remove you.

Contact messages

When you use the contact form we receive your name, your email address, your message and the page you sent it from. We use them to answer you.

How forms are handled

Form submissions go to our own form server in the European Union, which stores them and forwards them to our inbox by email. Every form also contains a hidden field that only spam bots fill in. Your browser keeps its own copy of a reservation or Nightlist sign-up in local storage on your device. We cannot see that copy, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.

We handle reservations and contact messages because you ask us to (GDPR article 6(1)(b)), and Nightlist addresses on the basis of your consent (article 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

Visitor statistics

We count visits with Umami, an open-source analytics tool that we run on our own server in the European Union. It sets no cookies and does not store your IP address. We see totals such as page views, referring sites, browser, device type and country, plus events such as opening the contact form or sending a form. We cannot identify you from these statistics.

Cookies

sonsoh.com sets no cookies: no tracking cookies, no advertising cookies and no social media pixels. That is why you do not see a cookie banner. The only thing stored on your device is the local copy of a reservation or Nightlist sign-up described above.

Other services this site uses

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). Like any web host, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver the pages and keep the service secure.
  • Fonts: our fonts load from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them, so Google receives your IP address.
  • Email: form messages reach us, and our replies reach you, through our email provider.
  • Payments: there are none on sonsoh.com. When your Founders number is ready to ship you receive a payment link, and the privacy policy of that payment provider applies to the payment.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep form data only as long as we need it for the purpose you gave it for: reservations until your order has been handled, including the trial and warranty period, or until you cancel; Nightlist addresses until you unsubscribe; contact messages as long as needed to help you. We delete it sooner when you ask.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to or limit its use, receive a copy, and withdraw your consent at any time. Send your request through the contact form and we will respond within one month. You can also file a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Changes

If the site starts doing something new with your data, we update this page first. The date at the top shows the current version.
