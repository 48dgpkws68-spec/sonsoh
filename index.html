FAQ

Good questions.

Will they stay in all night? +

Yes. NIGHT sits flush inside the concha with dual-flange silicone tips in three sizes. There's nothing sticking out for a pillow to catch, and at 2.9 g there's barely anything to feel. Most testers forget they're wearing them before they fall asleep.

Can I sleep on my side? +

That's exactly what NIGHT was designed for. The housing is fully recessed. We call it the pillow test: turn over, press your ear into the pillow, feel nothing. If you can feel them, use your 30-night trial.

What is shungite, exactly? +

Shungite is a rare, nearly pure-carbon mineral found only in Karelia, estimated to be around two billion years old. It naturally contains fullerene structures and is one of the few minerals studied for electromagnetic shielding as a conductive carbon material. We build a core of genuine shungite into every NIGHT earbud, a piece of deep time in a device built for deep sleep.

Is Bluetooth on all night? +

No. NIGHT uses Bluetooth Ultra-Low Energy, which transmits at a small fraction of a phone's power, and the sleep timer switches the radio off entirely once you're asleep. For everyone who prefers to keep their nights analog, that low-transmission design plus the shungite core is the whole point of NIGHT.

How long does the battery last? +

Up to six hours of continuous playback per night, and the case holds three additional full charges, about a week of nights. The LED display on the case shows the exact percentage remaining, and USB-C tops everything up fast.

When does it ship? +

The Founders Batch of 500 numbered pairs is the first to ship; we'll email you the date as soon as it is confirmed. Reserving today costs nothing: you'll receive a payment link when your number is ready to ship, and your 30-night trial starts on delivery, not on order.

Returns & warranty? +

Sleep with NIGHT for 30 nights. If it isn't the best sleep purchase you've made, return it for a full refund. No questions, no restocking games. Every pair carries a 2-year warranty.
